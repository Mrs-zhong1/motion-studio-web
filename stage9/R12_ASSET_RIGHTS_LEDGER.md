### Required entries for every distinct distributed media item
`asset_id, template_ids, source_path_or_url, sha256, creator_or_owner, license_identifier, proof_url_or_contract, allowed_redistribution, attribution_required, reviewer, reviewed_date`.

**Hard rules:** 
- An item with `UNKNOWN` ownership/license **blocks commercial template distribution**. 
- Model, image and font rights are considered separately; passing one does not clear others.
- A file's existence in the repo does not establish ownership or authorization.
- A permissive dependency license may still have required notice obligations.
- If license proof is unavailable, replace the asset with an original or properly licensed equivalent, re-run screenshot and export tests, then review again.

### Deployment separation
Public Pages is an **engineering review copy**, not an assertion that its assets may be sold. Do not merge/market until itemized approval is evidenced and linked by the release checker.
